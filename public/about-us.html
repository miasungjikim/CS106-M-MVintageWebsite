ABOUT US

We Are M&M

We’re a small team of vintage-obsessed designers building modern pieces with old-soul charm. From floral camp shirts to bow-neck wool sets and tapestry belts, our collections blend timeless silhouettes with easy, everyday wear.

Our Story

We started as weekend thrifters and pattern hunters, sketching from heirloom garments and family photo albums. What began as a tiny market stall grew into this shop—still independent, still detail-driven, and forever in love with the romance of vintage.

Care & Keep

Clothes should last. We provide care tips with each piece and offer simple repair guidance so your favorites stay in rotation for years.
